\begin{abstract}
We study exact persisted generated CYCLE membership using physical-event-preserving graph, cellular, simplicial and fixed-summary inputs. The original 4 prospectively registered simulator worlds are now described retrospectively as the primary pilot. A separate prospective replication evaluated 30 previously selected states on 30 newly registered realizations of the same controlled generator; all 900 prediction blocks were bound before the separate metric-label join. AP is averaged over all settings within world and then equally over worlds. Fixed-summary GBDT mean AP was 0.9164 (marginal 95\% paired-world interval [0.8716, 0.9572]), compared with 0.7009 for the summary neural control. All prespecified neural-minus-GBDT mean contrasts were negative; the complete conditional signed-rank family is reported with Holm correction. Per-world reversals remain visible. This replication concerns fixed selected states and one generator template. Full-corpus neural learning/stability and feature/readout/tuning parity remain unresolved; the local complex paths are adaptations. Development temporal-span evidence motivates a shortcut hypothesis, which replication alone does not establish causally. These observations do not establish broader financial efficacy or population precision.
\end{abstract}
\section{Question, contribution and related work}

Our question concerns exact membership in generated persisted cycle tuples, including SAR and non-SAR annotations. The comparison asks whether these particular retained representation/readout paths add measured recovery performance beyond fixed summaries. It does not estimate criminal activity. The observation contract retains individual physical events, their directions, native attributes and candidate masks; an aggregation that merges parallel transfers changes this task.

AMLSim is a synthetic transaction simulator credited to Suzumura and Kanezashi at the retained revision \citep{AMLSIM}. Weber et al. motivate synthetic graph-learning study in anti-money laundering \citep{WEBER2018}. CW networks \citep{CWN2021} and message passing simplicial networks \citep{MPSN2021} supply background for cellular and simplicial ideas. Our arms use disclosed local constructions and small readouts; they are adaptations, not faithful reproductions or refutations of those papers. Feature extraction paired with boosting is relevant prior work \citep{GFP2024}, with different tasks, features and populations. This is narrow background coverage; no published benchmark score is a measurement in this study.

The current simulator evidence is separate from earlier exploratory AMLworld, Elliptic and structural/engineering investigations. Historical repository metrics do not become current efficacy evidence. The original large AMLworld population design remains unresolved. Neither a new source nor a finite simulator comparison supplies missing population support for that design.

Dataset scope. Real-world AML annotation is constrained by confidentiality and incomplete detection. U.S. SAR protections in 31 U.S.C. \S{} 5318(g)(2) restrict disclosure revealing suspicious-transaction reporting \citep{SAR_CONFIDENTIALITY}; this does not establish a blanket prohibition on all deidentified bank transactions. Altman et al. (2023) motivate synthetic AML benchmarks with complete generated annotations \citep{ALTMAN2023}, rather than verified criminal ground truth. Our earlier HI-Small audit reconciled 54 supplied CYCLE blocks involving 271 bank-qualified accounts. Its then-used any-transaction-intersection labeling rule produced 7082 positive candidates, an amplification of the annotation target rather than that many independent laundering rings. That historical rule differs from the current exact physical-event-tuple membership target. In the inspected Elliptic++ address-to-address release \citep{ELLIPTIC2023}, the weak-component sizes were 7 and 822935; all eligible labeled wallets lay in the larger component. The address edge file lacked event timestamps, so feature-snapshot times could not define a prospective topology split. These are limitations of those inspected files and our specified target/custody requirements. They establish neither transaction-graph acyclicity nor that Elliptic++, other releases, or other financial benchmarks are generally unsuitable. The controlled AMLSim panel is a scoped experimental choice, not the only possible dataset.

\section{Data, target and candidate census}

The fixed controlled mechanism uses 32 accounts over 120 native simulator steps, 6 CYCLE templates, 2 noncycle alert templates and background transfers. It is based on pinned IBM AMLSim source with disclosed controlled-input, Java runtime and observation/persistence variants. The upstream revision and credits appear in the data card. Source code licensing does not grant release of the retained worlds or model states. Accounts and scheduling templates are shared design choices across realizations; seed changes do not demonstrate independent institutions.

Let a directed simple account cycle be an ordered distinct-account sequence modulo cyclic rotation, with length from 2 through 12. For each directed arc, take every physical persisted transfer with matching endpoints. The candidate frame is the Cartesian product of these arc-event sets for every cycle. Thus parallel equal-content transfers remain distinct; reversing direction is a different cycle. A candidate is a world-specific physical-event tuple, not an aggregated edge or an account identity. Self-loops remain in full-world context but are outside the simple candidate-cycle length frame. Cash-in/out rows are excluded from the candidate arc census. Guards reject more than 128 noncash events or 10000 candidate tuples without clipping or replacement.

A positive tuple equals one complete persisted generated CYCLE instance, reconciled through finalized membership/scheduling, arc attempts and native persisted events. Both SAR statuses are included. A nonmember is a complement of these exact annotations, not verified legitimate activity. Failed/incomplete intentions cannot silently become successful positives. Generation reconciliation accesses annotations, while scoring accesses only allowlisted observations/topology. Custody is local to one user; no externally sealed-label or independently witnessed execution claim follows.

The original pilot counts remain in its separate data card and tables. Every new replication realization had an actual census of 78 candidate tuples and 6 exact members. These observed counts were outcomes, not acceptance criteria imposed on new draws. Candidates overlap in accounts/transfers and are not independent population units. Full per-world counts, secondary metrics and confusion endpoints are exported; no pilot census was imposed on replication.

Time input is the native integer step divided by the configured number of steps; it is not a financial wall-clock time. Amount input is log1p of the actual persisted native amount. The retained Java logger truncates emitted positive double amounts toward zero at cents; reconciliation uses that persistence convention rather than nearest-cent rounding. No currency conversion, conservation, time-respecting flow or criminal interpretation is inferred.

\section{Representations and actual model equations}

For a physical event from account u to v, the cellular boundary column has -1 at u and +1 at v, with a zero column for a loop. The candidate face boundary selects its directed cycle events. Exact integer boundary closure satisfies B\textsubscript{1}B\textsubscript{2}=0. Every physical event retains a separate coordinate. The simplicial representation replaces each event by account \(\to\) source port \(\to\) event vertex \(\to\) target port \(\to\) account; a fan triangulates the selected subdivided perimeter. Spokes are auxiliary geometry, not financial transfers. Native event observations occur once at event vertices. The closure and identity checks concern this construction; external persistent-homology parity remains unavailable.

Write \(\sigma\) for tanh and L for a trainable affine map, with independently named maps below. The cellular arm computes node h\textsubscript{v}=\(\sigma\)(L\textsubscript{n}m\textsubscript{v}), initial event h\textsubscript{e}\textsuperscript{0}=\(\sigma\)(L\textsubscript{e}[t\textsubscript{e},a\textsubscript{e},s\textsubscript{e}]), and face h\(\mathcal{f}\)\textsuperscript{0}=\(\sigma\)(L\(\mathcal{f}\)k). Its lower message is |B\textsubscript{1}|\textsuperscript{T}h\textsubscript{v}, plus separately transformed source and target states. Its upper message is |B\textsubscript{2}|h\(\mathcal{f}\)\textsuperscript{0}. The updated event state is \(\sigma\)(L\textsubscript{u}[h\textsubscript{e}\textsuperscript{0}, lower+directional, upper]); the face update is \(\sigma\)(L\(\mathcal{f}\)\textsubscript{u}[h\(\mathcal{f}\)\textsuperscript{0}, |B\textsubscript{2}|\textsuperscript{T}h\textsubscript{e}]). The scalar logit reads the updated face plus the mean of all world-event states. In the Hasse control, the lower message is separately transformed source and target states instead of |B\textsubscript{1}|\textsuperscript{T}h\textsubscript{v}; the additional directional maps and face/event readout remain. This local contrast is descriptive and does not isolate a causal topology mechanism.

The simplicial arm encodes vertex type/mask/event observations and edge-relation indicators. It computes h\textsubscript{v}=\(\sigma\)(L\textsubscript{v}v), h\textsubscript{e}=\(\sigma\)(L\textsubscript{e}[\(\sigma\)(L\textsubscript{r}e), |B\textsubscript{1}|\textsuperscript{T}h\textsubscript{v}]), h\textsubscript{v}'=\(\sigma\)(L\textsubscript{v}\textsubscript{u}[h\textsubscript{v}, |B\textsubscript{1}|h\textsubscript{e}]), and h\(\mathcal{f}\)=\(\sigma\)(L\(\mathcal{f}\)\textsubscript{u}[\(\sigma\)(L\(\mathcal{f}\)f), |B\textsubscript{2}|\textsuperscript{T}h\textsubscript{e}]). Its logit reads mean face plus mean updated vertex states. Absolute incidence removes basis-sign information from these paths; coordinate invariance does not establish signed-cochain reasoning or original MPSN semantics.

The conventional directed edge GNN computes separate incoming/outgoing event messages from endpoint node states and native event features, sums them at accounts, and updates node states. It encodes source-updated event states. Its final affine readout concatenates the masked mean of central node states, the masked mean of selected event states, and candidate length; masked denominators include the executed small positive epsilon. Whole-world topology and event attributes remain available to the neural arms.

The fixed-summary input contains cycle length; min/mean/max/population-SD of selected time and log amount; their spans; min/mean/max central full-world in/out degree; and min/mean/max selected directed-pair multiplicity. These are lossy summaries of legitimate observations, not identical tensors to graph inputs. GBDT receives unnormalized float64 summaries. The summary network is affine--ReLU--affine--ReLU--affine with input dimension 20, hidden width 81 in each hidden layer, and output dimension 1. The original training-only population mean/SD transform is applied in float64, exact constant columns use scale one, then neural tensors become float32. No transform is fit on validation or holdout rows.

Here the cycle enumeration already supplies a simple-cycle candidate frame to every arm. The local cellular and simplicial adaptations use the specific subdivisions, absolute-incidence paths and readouts given above, with whole-world observation context retained. \section{Chronology, prediction boundary and estimand}

The original W1--W4 panel was registered as a 4-world study. Its 120 blocks and results were exposed before the descriptor ``Pre-Registered Primary Pilot'' was assigned; that pilot descriptor is retrospective. It remains descriptive, with no retrospectively added pilot p-values or confidence intervals. W5--W34 form a separate prospective replication registered before generation, using the same 30 previously selected states and unchanged transform, thresholds and staged GBDT policy. There was one attempt per draw, no replacement or successful-block rescoring, and no fitting or selection from replication outcomes. All 900 blocks were hash-bound before the scorer exited and a separate process joined labels. The two panels are never pooled for confirmation.

Generation and annotation reconciliation are annotation-aware. Scoring opens its allowlisted observation/topology inputs only; this local prediction boundary operates under the same OS user. It does not supply externally sealed labels, independent institutional custody or cold external review. The statistical verification used a distinct arithmetic implementation in the same local session after the conclusions were exposed.

For descending distinct logit thresholds, grouped AP is the sum of recall increments times cumulative precision; exactly equal logits enter together. AUROC gives half credit to positive/nonmember ties, BCE is mean stable logit binary cross-entropy, and confusion counts use raw logit \(\geq\)0 without calibration. AP is unavailable without positives; AUROC is unavailable for a single class. Null required AP blocks the complete primary aggregate and inference rather than dropping a world. All required primary endpoints were defined here. Secondary metrics and all counts remain descriptive and are retained for every setting.

The primary observable first averages 5 separately computed state APs within each arm/world and then equally weights all 30 replication worlds. It is neither candidate-pooled AP nor AP of averaged logits. Initializations condition on the same trained-data history and do not multiply independent realizations. The realization of this fixed generator is the analysis unit; the original AMLworld population design and its learning, custody and precision requirements remain unresolved.

\section{Conditional statistical protocol}

All 5 prespecified neural-minus-GBDT contrasts use paired world means. Rank arithmetic rounds differences to 12 decimal places, omits exact zeros (wilcox), and assigns midranks to tied absolute differences. A dynamic program enumerates the conditional sign distribution on integer doubled ranks. The two-sided p-value is min(1, 2 \(\times\) min(inclusive lower tail, inclusive upper tail)); integer tail counts and denominators are retained. Holm adjustment covers the full family at \(\alpha\)=0.05. The null is conditional sign symmetry about zero under the assumed independence of generator realizations; it is not a test specifically of the arithmetic mean contrast. Shared design and distinct seeds alone do not establish these assumptions.

The paired world bootstrap uses 20000 resamples of 30 worlds with replacement and the same indices across all arms and contrasts. The published arithmetic utility uses PCG64(2026101103) and unrounded world means/differences; percentile endpoints use linear quantiles at 0.025 and 0.975. The 95\% intervals are marginal, not simultaneous or multiplicity-adjusted, and are not a power or precision guarantee. All interval widths are reported.

Chance references come from each realized candidate census: a constant-score AP is m/N, while uniform random tie-free expected AP is H\_N/N + (m-1)(N-H\_N)/(N(N-1)) for the nondegenerate census, with H\_N the harmonic sum. Here all realized censuses share N=78 and m=6, giving 0.076923077 and 0.124159693 respectively. These are expectations, not hard lower bounds on any arm. Candidates and settings are not independent worlds.

\section{Negative findings, failure analysis and limitations}

The weak frozen graph-arm AP is a legitimate retained outcome. It cannot establish intrinsic architectural inferiority because full-corpus optimization and finite stability are unresolved. Conversely, successful microfits do not repair that qualification. A strong baseline is evidence that legitimate summaries capture useful signal in these small generator realizations; deleting that signal to make another representation competitive would change the question.

Existing development column-permutation and native-span investigations point to schedule cues of this generator. They remain development-only evidence, not a new holdout temporal model or a causal intervention. The study did not establish matched causal ablations, sensitivity, external PH backend parity, broader/OOD validation or financial generalization.
