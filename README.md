# Financial Collusion Ring Detection

Toporing studies transaction-preserving graph, cellular and simplicial inputs for exact generated cycle membership recovery. The manuscript keeps the original W1–W4 results as a disclosed retrospectively described pilot and adds a separate prospectively registered W5–W34 replication with unchanged selected states.

Replication equal-world AP was 0.9164 for fixed-summary GBDT and 0.7009 for the summary neural control. Every prespecified neural-minus-GBDT mean contrast was negative; complete conditional signed-rank/Holm results and marginal paired-world intervals accompany every arm/world/setting endpoint. Per-world reversals remain visible. Comparative neural learning/stability, feature/readout/tuning fairness and a causal temporal-shortcut mechanism remain unresolved. The finding is conditional on one small controlled generator and these selected states, with no broader financial efficacy or population-precision guarantee.
